# Phase 5 — Regulator adapters (C: ASA UK, JDP FR) and ownership & repair (E)

## What was built

**Regulators (differentiator C, ADR-003)** — `src/lib/regulators/`
- `adapter.ts`: `RegulatorAdapter { id, name, country, searchUrl, search(brand, fetcher), fetchRuling(url, fetcher) }`, `RulingCandidate`, `Ruling { url, title, advertiser, date, outcome: upheld | not_upheld | informally_resolved | unknown, outcomeText, note?, excerpt, fetchedAt }`. `getRegulatorPage()` turns every failure into a `RegulatorUnavailableError` whose message is the shown skip reason (robots verbatim, `ASA unreachable (HTTP 500)`, network error); `RegulatorParseError` for pages without the known structure. English and French date parsing.
- `asa-uk.ts`: `GET https://www.asa.org.uk/search.html?q=<brand>` → `/rulings/…html` hits only (news and guidance ignored), with listed date and outcome; ruling page → advertiser from "ASA Ruling on …", outcome and date from the meta line, excerpt = first 300 chars from "Background". "Upheld in part" ⇒ `upheld` with note.
- `jdp-fr.ts`: `GET https://www.jdp-pub.org/avis/?search_avis=<brand>` (the listing's own search box; `?s=` does not filter it, probed live) → `/avis/<slug>/` cards; decision page → advertiser = heading, outcome heading, "Avis publié le <date>", excerpt from "1. La plainte". Outcome order: partiellement fondée ⇒ upheld + note; non fondée / rejetée ⇒ not_upheld; fondée ⇒ upheld.
- `match.ts`: normalisation (lowercase, diacritics, punctuation, `&`, trailing legal suffixes from `config/wikidata.json`, trailing qualifiers UK/Europe/International/Holdings… from `config/regulators.json`), `t/a` splitting, three-way `scored | possible | none` (see C-3 below). `searchQuery()` strips the legal suffix ("Oatly AB" ⇒ "Oatly").
- `check.ts`: `checkRegulator()` — hits whose listed name is not even a whole-word match are never fetched; scored first, then possible, at most `maxRulings` (5) pages; scored ⇒ `regulator.ruling` signal (axis `track`, `entity_name` verbatim, receipt = ruling page, `llm_used: false`); possible ⇒ `PossibleMatch { scored: false }` + a could-not-check line, never a signal. `rulingWeightHint()` = base × upheld × recent (≤ 3 years), values in the new `config/weights.json` (`regulatorRuling` section only; Phase 6 adds the rest).
- Pipeline steps `src/lib/pipeline/steps/regulators.ts`: "Checking ASA (UK) rulings", "Checking JDP (France) decisions", one per adapter, after evidence (brief Section 5 order). A failure skips only that step. Both add the ADR-003 coverage line ("only the UK ASA and the French JDP were searched"); `runPipeline` now de-duplicates could-not-check lines. `PipelineOutcome.regulator_possible`, `ctx.rulings`.

**Repair (differentiator E)** — `src/lib/repair/`
- `applicability.ts`: entity industry (Wikidata P452 label on the confirmed candidate, now passed as `run.entity`) in `config/discovery.json` `nonDurableIndustries` ⇒ not applicable. Unknown industry ⇒ applicable.
- `brand-pages.ts`: rules `repair_manual`, `spare_parts`, `warranty_years`, `repair_program` over every fetched page's text lines and link texts (nav links included). One protective signal per rule (`repair.manual_found`, `repair.spare_parts_found`, `repair.warranty_found` with the longest warranty, `repair.program_found`); none on any page ⇒ `repair.no_repair_info_found`, excerpt "Pages scanned: <title> (<url>); …". `/repair`, `/warranty`, `/support` were already discovery heuristics (Phase 2), so no extra fetch is made.
- `ifixit.ts`: device-category endpoint `/api/2.0/wikis/CATEGORY/<brand>` (search endpoint is robots-disallowed, C-2); category title must equal the brand; signals `repair.ifixit_devices`, and for the last 3 device sub-categories `repair.ifixit_guides` (guide titles in excerpt) and `repair.ifixit_score`. 404 ⇒ could-not-check "iFixit lists no device category named …"; unreachable ⇒ could-not-check line, page rules still run.
- Step `src/lib/pipeline/steps/repair.ts` "Checking repair information": not applicable ⇒ `skipped: not applicable: …`, zero signals, one could-not-check line, `repair_axis: "not_applicable"`; no pages and nothing from iFixit ⇒ `skipped: no fetched page to scan`.

**Fixtures** — `fixtures/sources/` (README there): all **real captures** by the new `npm run record:sources` (`scripts/record-sources.ts`, robots-guarded, 1 req/s/host, project User-Agent, TLS verified via the proxy CA), 2026-10-21T00:09Z. Loaded offline by `src/lib/fetch/source-fixtures.ts` (per-URL capture date), wired into `services.ts` and the test `OfflineFetcher`.
- Oatly (food fixture brand) has a real ASA ruling: *Oatly UK Ltd t/a Oatly*, 26 January 2022, upheld in part. So **no fourth fixture brand was needed** for DoD item 4.
- Mammut, Fairphone: real empty ASA and JDP searches. No fixture brand has a JDP decision, so the JDP parser is proved on a **regulator-only** real case, TotalEnergies (search + 3 decisions). The PLAN's `asa-ambiguous` fixture is the real ASA search for "Shell" (+ 3 ruling pages): two exact-name rulings and *Shell Energy UK* (possible). These are not fixture brands (no brand pages, nothing scored for them in a run); they only exercise the parsers and the matcher.
- iFixit: real JSON for *Fairphone* and its *Fairphone 5 / 6 / 6plus* categories (Fairphone 5 and 6: repairability score 10). iFixit answered 404 for *Mammut* (not recorded, served as 404 offline).
- robots.txt of the three hosts recorded.

**Docs** — METHODOLOGY Sections 9 (regulators) and 10 (repair); LIMITATIONS sections for C and E; LATER rows (search pagination, iFixit search API); CHALLENGES C-2, C-3.

## Deviations, skipped, and why

- **C-2: iFixit search endpoint not used.** `robots.txt` disallows `/api/*` except `/api/2.0/categories*`, `guides*`, `wikis*`; ADR-010 keeps repair sources under the robots guard. The plan's `/api/2.0/search/<brand>` would always be skipped, so the allowed category endpoint is used. Raised in `docs/CHALLENGES.md`.
- **C-3: matching rule.** Deliverable 3's literal "whole word ⇒ scored" contradicts criterion 5.3 (Patagonia Provisions ⇒ possible; Pata ⇒ no match). Implemented the criteria: scored only on name equality after normalisation. Documented outcome: "Shell" vs "Shell Energy Retail" ⇒ **possible**. Raised in `docs/CHALLENGES.md`.
- **JDP search parameter** is `search_avis`, not `?s=` (which returns the unfiltered listing).
- **Fixture paths**: `fixtures/sources/regulators/<slug>/asa.html|jdp.html`, `repair/fairphone/ifixit-*.json` instead of inside `fixtures/brands/<slug>/`: the brand manifests have one capture date and their `files` are read as brand pages by Phase 2–4 helpers; regulator pages are not brand pages.
- **Warranty phrasing extended** with numbers in words ("Five-year warranty", Fairphone) and "guarantee" ("Our 2-year quality guarantee", Mammut); "repair tutorial(s)" counts as a repair guide; "send it in to our service centers" counts as a repair service. All in METHODOLOGY 10.2.
- **Outcome words in labels** are the regulator's own ("Upheld in part", "Plainte rejetée"), inside the ADR-008 template "Ruling found naming <entity verbatim>".
- Existing tests that pinned the full default progress list (`tests/unit/pipeline/skip.test.ts`, `tests/unit/claims/fixtures.test.ts`) gained the three new rows; offline, a brand with no recorded regulator search shows `skipped: ASA unreachable (HTTP 404)` (same convention as Wayback).
- No LLM is used anywhere in Phase 5; every signal has `llm_used: false`. No score is computed (Phase 6).
