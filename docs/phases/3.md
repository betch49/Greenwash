# Phase 3 — Claim-language engine (F) with substantiation, evidence-axis rules, methodology doc

Builder note, 2026-10-20.

## What was built

**Claim rules (`src/lib/claims/rules/`).** One module per family, each exporting `{ id, family, description, test(sentence, { next? }) → RuleMatch | null }`. A `RuleMatch` carries the first matched phrase and the spans of every trigger phrase.

| id | family | matches when |
|---|---|---|
| `vague` | vague | eco-friendly, earth-friendly, eco-conscious, conscious + noun, responsibly made, green + noun, natural + noun, clean + noun, low-impact…, or "sustainable" used bare (not after more/most/fully/100%…, not in a proper name such as "Sustainable Development Goals") |
| `absolute` | absolute | zero impact, impact-free, 100% / fully / completely sustainable or natural, the most sustainable…; "zero waste" only when not qualified ("to landfill"…) |
| `offset` | offset | carbon / climate / e-waste neutral, net zero, carbon positive… in the present tense (no future cue) and with no reduction figure (number + unit next to a reduction verb) in the sentence or the next |
| `baseline_less_comparison` | baseline-less comparison | more sustainable, less plastic, lower impact, better for the planet… with no "than"/"compared with"/"baseline", no percentage and no year |
| `future_only` | future-only | we will, by 20XX, aim to, committed to, our goal is… about an environmental topic, with no progress figure (number + unit next to since/already/achieved/reduced/"in 20XX"…) in the sentence or the next |

`rules/index.ts` is the registry (`CLAIM_RULES`, `RULE_IDS`). All phrases are data in `src/lib/claims/lexicon.ts`; the rule modules only compile them (word-bounded, case-insensitive unless marked).

**Sentence splitter (`split.ts`).** Deterministic: lines stay apart; within a line a sentence ends at `. ! ? …` + whitespace + a non-lowercase character, except after a known abbreviation or an initial.

**Substantiation (`substantiation.ts`).** Window = the matched sentence and the following two. Markers: number + unit (%, percent, t, tCO2e, tonnes, kg, kWh, MWh, litres, m³…), a year, a named standard/certifier (the plan's list plus RDS, Fair Wear, TCO Certified), or a content link whose text appears in the window and matches /report|data|methodology|footnote|study|assessment/. The claim's own trigger spans are blanked first, so "100% sustainable" is not substantiated by its "100%" and "net zero by 2040" not by its "2040".

**Engine (`engine.ts`).** `scanPage(pageText, { url, fetchedAt, links }) → { signals, stats, hits }`.
- One signal per distinct sentence and rule: axis `claims`, kind `claim.unsupported` (no marker) or `claim.supported`, label from `wording.ts`, excerpt = the matched sentence (≤ 300 chars, cut around the phrase), receipt from the page record, `llm_used: false`, `weight_hint: 1` (placeholder; points per kind come from `config/weights.json` in Phase 6).
- `ClaimStats`: per rule matches, unsupported, `per1000`, `unsupportedPer1000`; repeated sentences count every time. `mergeStats()` sums pages and recomputes rates over the total words.
- `classify(sentence, next?)` runs all rules; questions never match.

**Evidence rules (`evidence-rules.ts`)**, over all pages of a run (content and chrome links):
- `labels_claimed`: certifier names in the page text ⇒ `labels_claimed[]` and one could-not-check entry each ("<name>: label claimed on page; not verified in v1 (<url>)."). Never a signal.
- `impact_report_link`: link text or href matching report/impact/sustainability, and a PDF or a year. Year from link text, then file name, then path; years after the fetch year ignored; newest wins (PDF on a tie). ⇒ `evidence.report_found` (≤ 2 years old), `evidence.stale_report` (> 2), `evidence.report_found_undated` (undated PDF only), `evidence.no_report_found` (none; receipt = first page, excerpt = titles scanned).
- `absolute_emissions_present`: number + tCO2e / t CO2 / tonnes (of) CO2e, excluding avoided/offset/per-unit sentences ⇒ `evidence.absolute_emissions_found`.
- `supplier_list_present`: link text supplier list / factory list / our factories / supply chain map ⇒ `evidence.supplier_list_found`.

**Pipeline.** Two new steps after page fetching: "Checking claim language" (`steps/claims.ts`, dedupes the same sentence across pages, stores `ctx.claimStats` and `ctx.claimHits`) and "Checking published evidence" (`steps/evidence.ts`, stores `ctx.evidence`). Both end `skipped: no fetched page to scan` when there are no pages. `PipelineOutcome` gains `claim_stats`. Signals and stats are returned, not yet persisted (the report record is Phase 6).

**Copy.** `wording.ts` gains `steps.claims/evidence`, `claimFamilyNames`, `signalLabels`, `excerpts`, `couldNotCheck.labelClaimed`. `src/lib/copy/forbidden.ts`: `FORBIDDEN_TERMS` (ADR-008 list plus liar/deceive/fraudulent forms; "greenwash…" forbidden unless it is the product name or "greenwashing risk …"), `findForbidden()`, `passesWordingFilter()` (for the Phase 7 summary).

**`docs/METHODOLOGY.md`** v1: pipeline from page to sentences, each rule id with its pattern in words and two hits + one miss, substantiation, signal kinds, ClaimStats, the four evidence rules, a weights placeholder pointing at `config/weights.json`, and the rules' limits.

**Fixture results** (all pages of each real brand): Mammut 10 matches / 10 unsupported, report 2025 found; Fairphone 18 / 11, report 2025 found, label Fairtrade; Oatly 94 / 53, report FY2025 found. None of the three real captures publishes an absolute tCO2e figure or a supplier-list link on the fetched pages; the fictional Northwind fixture exercises those two rules.

## Skipped / deviations, and why

- **`claim.supported` kind.** The plan names only `claim.unsupported`. A substantiated hit still gets a signal (kind `claim.supported`) so the report can show it with its sentence and Phase 6 can weight it lower; the per-family split lives in `ClaimStats` (the brief's claims-axis input), not in the kind.
- **Trigger words masked before substantiation.** Not spelled out in the plan; without it every "100% sustainable" and every "by 2030" target would substantiate itself.
- **Questions never match** ("How sustainable is the Fairphone 3?"): a question asserts nothing.
- **Lexicon narrowing found on real pages:** future-only "will" needs a subject form (we will, our products will, will be carbon/net…) and an environmental topic; "greener"/"cleaner" need a noun or "is …" ("cleaner, richer sound" is not a claim); "plan to" only as "we plan to". "percent" and litres/m³ count as units (Northwind and Oatly write "percent").
- **Pipeline steps added now.** Phase 3 lists the engine, not the wiring; the two steps make the engine run on every analysis and let 3.4 be checked end to end. Nothing is persisted or shown beyond the progress line until Phase 6/8.
- **`weight_hint: 1`** on every Phase 3 signal: no weights file exists yet (Phase 6 owns `config/weights.json`).
- **Known noise, left visible:** quotes and advocacy text match (Oatly's food-policy essay, Mammut's paragraph critical of carbon neutrality, Fairphone's "By 2050, an estimated 120 million tons of e-waste will be generated"). Every match is shown with its sentence; METHODOLOGY Section 7 says so.
- **CHALLENGES C-1** (`docs/CHALLENGES.md`): a year in a neighbouring target sentence currently substantiates a claim (Northwind "We are carbon neutral."). Implemented as the plan says; proposal and effect recorded for the director.

### Defects (numbered; all must be fixed in rework round 1)

1. **Substantiation marks targets and bare dates as evidence (C-1 upheld, ADR-014).** Reproduced with `scanPage`: "We are carbon neutral." + "We will be net zero … by 2040." + "We aim to cut emissions by 2030." ⇒ `claim.supported` (markers `year=2040, year=2030`); "Our jackets are eco-friendly. Founded in 1998, we make gear." ⇒ supported (`year=1998`); "We are the most sustainable brand. Shop the 2025 collection." ⇒ supported; "We are committed to 100% renewable electricity." ⇒ supported by its own target figure; "We will be a sustainable company by 2030." ⇒ the vague hit is supported by the sentence's own target year. Implement ADR-014: (a) a year is never a marker on its own, only recorded next to a number + unit in the same window sentence and never after by/until/till/before/no later than/towards/toward/ahead of; (b) a window sentence that itself matches `future_only` contributes no marker; (c) for a `future_only` hit the matched sentence contributes no number + unit marker. Add the ADR-014 cases of criterion 3.2 (PLAN, amended) to `tests/unit/claims/substantiation.test.ts`; the two original 3.2 cases and "Our emissions were 1,200 tCO2e" must still pass. Target words go in `lexicon.ts`, not in code.
2. **`future_only` fires on third-party statistics (ADR-015).** "By 2050, an estimated 120 million tons of e-waste will be generated" (Fairphone fixture) is not a commitment. When the only trigger in the sentence is "by 20XX", require a commitment subject cue in the same sentence (we, our, us, the company, the brand name passed in by the pipeline; list in `lexicon.ts`). Tests: that sentence ⇒ no match; "By 2030 we will halve our packaging waste." ⇒ `future_only`; "We will be net zero by 2040." unchanged. Fixture test 3.4 must still find ≥ 1 signal per brand.
3. **Docs and label out of sync with the ruling.** Update `docs/METHODOLOGY.md` Section 3 (markers, "targets are not evidence", the two new exclusions) and Section 2 `future_only` (subject cue), and `signalLabels.claimSupported` in `wording.ts` (it currently names "year" as a marker). `methodology-sync` and `forbidden` tests must stay green.
4. **Phase note.** Append a "Rework round 1" section to this file with the new test counts and the fixture results after the change (matches / unsupported per brand).

Signal labels use "risk signal", "unsupported claim", "no supporting data found on the fetched page", "label claimed on page; not verified in v1" only. The forbidden scan is an AST scan of every string literal in `src/`, stronger than ADR-008 asked; "deceive" and "liar" forms were added to the list, consistent with ADR-008's intent. `llm_used` is `false` on every Phase 3 signal, so the 50% cap is untouched by this phase.

## Rework round 1

Builder note, 2026-10-20. Fixes the four defects of the director review (ADR-014, ADR-015).

1. **Substantiation (ADR-014)** — `src/lib/claims/substantiation.ts`, lexicon `SUBSTANTIATION.targetPrepositions`.
   - A year is never a marker on its own. It is recorded (kind `year`, for display) only when the same window sentence has a number + unit, and never after by / until / till / before / no later than / towards / toward / ahead of. `supported` is true only for a number + unit, a certifier or an evidence link.
   - A window sentence that matches `future_only` (classified with the same next-sentence and brand context as the engine) contributes no marker. For a hit of another rule this includes the matched sentence.
   - For a `future_only` hit, its own sentence contributes no number + unit. Certifiers and evidence links there still count.
   - `findSubstantiation(sentences, i, spans, links, { ruleId, brand })`; the engine passes both.
   - Tests (`substantiation.test.ts`, new block "targets are not evidence"): every ADR-014 case of amended 3.2 (carbon neutral + two targets ⇒ unsupported; "Founded in 1998" ⇒ unsupported; "Shop the 2025 collection" ⇒ unsupported; "committed to 100% renewable electricity" ⇒ unsupported; "We will be a sustainable company by 2030." ⇒ vague and future_only both unsupported; "… Our emissions were 1,200 tCO2e." ⇒ supported). Also year/target-preposition markers, a future-only window sentence giving no marker (not even a certifier), and a future-only hit keeping its certifier. The original 3.2 cases still pass.
2. **future_only subject cue (ADR-015)** — `rules/future.ts`, lexicon `FUTURE.byYear`, `FUTURE.commitmentSubjects`, `FUTURE.brandSuffixes`. When "by 20XX" is the only trigger, the sentence must contain we / we're / we've / our / ours / us / the company / the brand / the group, or the run's brand name (full, and with legal suffixes removed: "Oatly AB" ⇒ "Oatly"). `RuleContext.brand`, `PageMeta.brand` and `classify(sentence, next, brand)` carry it; the claims step passes `ctx.run.brand`. Tests (`rules.test.ts`): the Fairphone e-waste statistic ⇒ no match; "By 2030 we will halve our packaging waste." ⇒ `future_only`; "We will be net zero by 2040." unchanged; brand name as subject, with suffix stripping.
