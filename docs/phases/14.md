## Property 14.2: the v2 counter-example (v1 stays default)

Removing an unsupported sentence never raises the share ((u−1)/(m−1) ≤ u/m), and the clamp holds (m < minClaims ⇒ 0; tested over 1,000 random sets). Removing a **supported** sentence lowers the share only when (u + w·s)/m ≤ w, i.e. never in practice: with u=5, s=1 the sub-score is 87.5; without the supported sentence (u=5, m=5) it is 100. The full property is pinned as `it.fails` in `tests/unit/score/monotonic.test.ts` (the suite fails if it ever passes), the counter-example as a plain test. Per the plan, `version: 1` ships; CHALLENGES C-14 offers options.
