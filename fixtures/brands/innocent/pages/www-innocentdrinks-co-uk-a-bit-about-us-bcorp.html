B Corp

what it means to be a B Corp

Being a B Corp is about more than just a nice certificate. It's a chance for us to step forward. B Corps are a movement of businesses that believe in balancing purpose and profit. As part of the B Corp community, we can learn a few tricks from other businesses and share some of the stuff we've learned too. We can also shout louder about the change we want to see in the world and inspire others to join us.

Being a B Corp is about much more than ticking boxes. We're on a mission to prove that business can be done differently. Not with focus on financial performance above all else, but with a genuine commitment to all the people who work here, all the people who drink our drinks. That means planting B Corp at the heart of our business and getting everyone behind it. 

B a part of it

We're in good company. There are thousands of other businesses who are part of the B Corp movement. Find out more about the movement and what you can do to get involved.
