we're innocent

We've been a proud B Corp since 2018, meaning we're officially using business as a force for good. we're a proud B Corp

We’ve always believed in doing business responsibly, from the farms that grow our ingredients to the biscuit tin in the office. We’ve been a proud B Corp since 2018, which means we're part of a group of businesses committed to being a force for good. In practice, that involves things like giving 10% of our profits to good causes, and building our own all-electric little drinks factory, the Blender. And we’ll carry on doing things like that for as long as we’re around.
