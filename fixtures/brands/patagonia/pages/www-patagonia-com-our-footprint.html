Our footprint

Everything we make has an impact on the planet. This page explains where our emissions come from, what we have changed, and what we still have to do.

Where our emissions come from

Around 95 percent of our carbon emissions come from our supply chain, mostly from making the fabrics used in our products. In fiscal year 2025 our total footprint was 186,000 tCO2e across scopes 1, 2 and 3, compared with 205,000 tCO2e in fiscal year 2022.

Our own stores, offices and distribution centers run on renewable electricity in the United States, which covers scope 2 emissions for those sites.

Materials

Our products are made from sustainable materials. In 2025, 88 percent of our line by weight was made with preferred materials, including recycled polyester, recycled nylon and organic cotton certified to the GOTS standard.

All of our down is certified to the Responsible Down Standard and our wool comes from farms certified to the Responsible Wool Standard (RWS).

Our new shell fabric is more sustainable and uses less water.

Climate

We are carbon neutral.

We will be net zero across our entire business by 2040.

We aim to cut the emissions of our supply chain in half by 2030, measured against a 2019 baseline, and we report progress every year in our impact report.

Packaging

Our packaging is 100% sustainable and completely natural.
