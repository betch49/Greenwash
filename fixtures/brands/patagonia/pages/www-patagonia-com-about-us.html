About us

We started as a small company making tools for climbers. Today we make clothing and gear for many outdoor sports, sold in stores and online in more than ten countries.

We are a certified B Corp and a member of 1% for the Planet: every year we give one percent of our sales to grassroots environmental groups.

Our approach to business is simple: build the best product, cause no unnecessary harm, and use business to protect nature. We are an eco-conscious, green company.

Ownership

The company's voting stock is held by a purpose trust, and its non-voting stock by a non-profit organisation that uses the profits not reinvested in the business to fight the environmental crisis.
