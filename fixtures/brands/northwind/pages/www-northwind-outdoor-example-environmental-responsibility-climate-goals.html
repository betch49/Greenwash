Our climate goals

Climate change is the biggest threat to the places we love. This page lists our climate targets and how we are tracking against them.

Targets

  • Reduce absolute scope 1 and 2 emissions by 80 percent by 2030 from a 2019 baseline.
  • Reduce scope 3 emissions from purchased goods and services by 55 percent per unit of product by 2030.
  • Reach net zero across the value chain by 2040.

Progress

Since 2019 we have cut scope 1 and 2 emissions by 71 percent, mainly by switching our US sites to renewable electricity. Scope 3 emissions per unit have fallen by 24 percent over the same period.

Our targets were validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) in 2023.

Offsets

Our products are climate neutral. We do not use offsets to meet our reduction targets, but we fund regenerative organic agriculture projects with our suppliers.
