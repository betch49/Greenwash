Supply chain responsibility

We do not own the factories that make our products, so we work with our suppliers to improve working conditions and environmental performance. Every finished-goods factory is audited before we place an order, and again at least every two years.

We publish the name and address of every finished-goods factory and of our main fabric mills in our supplier list, updated twice a year.

Fair Trade

Many of our products are Fair Trade Certified sewn. For every Fair Trade product we sell, we pay a premium that goes directly to the workers at the factory, who vote on how to spend it.

Living wages

We are committed to paying a living wage throughout our supply chain.

We measure the gap between current wages and a living wage at each strategic factory, using the Global Living Wage Coalition benchmarks, and we share the results with the factory and the workers' representatives.

Chemicals

Our fabrics are screened against a restricted substances list and many of our mills are bluesign system partners. We are phasing out PFAS from our water-repellent finishes and our gear is safe and natural.
