Lifetime Guarantee

We guarantee everything we make. If you are not satisfied with one of our products at the time you receive it, or if one of our products does not perform to your satisfaction, return it to the store you bought it from or to us for a repair, replacement or refund.

Damage due to wear and tear will be repaired at a reasonable charge.

This is in effect a lifetime warranty: there is no time limit on a claim for a defect in materials or workmanship, and you do not need a receipt to start a claim.

How to make a claim

Bring the item to one of our stores, or start a claim online. We will inspect it and tell you whether it will be repaired, replaced or refunded, usually within two weeks.
