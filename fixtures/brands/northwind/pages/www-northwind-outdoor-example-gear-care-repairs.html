Repairs

Repairing your gear keeps it in use longer and out of the landfill. Our repair centers fix tens of thousands of items every year, from broken zippers to torn fabric.

How to get a repair

  1. Start a repair request online and tell us what is wrong.
  2. Send it in for repair using the prepaid label, or drop it off at one of our stores.
  3. We repair it, or tell you if it cannot be repaired, and ship it back to you.

Fix it yourself

We publish free repair guides with step-by-step instructions for common fixes, and our parts store sells replacement parts such as buckles, zipper sliders and cord locks.

Cost

Repairs covered by our Lifetime Guarantee are free. Other repairs are charged at a reasonable fee, which we tell you before we start the work.
