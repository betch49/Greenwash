Improving well-being

Improving well-being

Access to hygiene and health is essential for quality of life and effective business operations. Essity delivers solutions that support dignity, comfort, and well-being. Together with our partners, we expand access, address key health challenges, and create lasting value through innovation, collaboration, and education.

Learn more

Climate action

Climate action

We reduce emissions across our operations and value chain in line with our science-based targets, and we develop lower-carbon solutions that support our customers' sustainability ambitions and contribute to a more sustainable future.

Learn more

Healthy ecosystems

Healthy ecosystems

We respect biodiversity and ecosystems by reducing resource use and promoting the responsible use of fiber, plastics, chemicals, and water.

Learn more

Empowered workforce

Empowered workforce

We invest in learning, leadership, and well-being to create a safe, inclusive environment where employees and partners can thrive.

Learn more

Responsible business practices

Responsible business practices

We uphold high standards of ethics, human rights, and transparency across our value chain and in the communities where we operate.

Learn more

Responsible sourcing

Responsible sourcing

We promote responsible sourcing across our value chain through supplier standards, due diligence, and engagement to reduce risk, build trust, support human rights, ethical business practices, and transparency.
