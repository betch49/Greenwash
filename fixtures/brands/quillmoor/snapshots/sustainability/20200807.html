Sustainability at Quillmoor

Quillmoor makes oat drinks and oat yogurts in our own dairy-free plant in the north of the country. We buy our oats from 31 family farms within 200 km of the plant.

Our oat drinks are carbon neutral. Our packaging is eco-friendly and fully recyclable. We're committed to regenerative farming on every partner farm.

The plant runs on electricity from a local wind cooperative, and we send our oat fibre to a nearby biogas facility.

Questions about our ingredients? Write to our customer team and we will answer within two working days.
