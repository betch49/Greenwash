Sustainability at Quillmoor

Quillmoor makes oat drinks and oat yogurts in our own dairy-free plant in the north of the country.

Our oat drinks are carbon neutral

The plant runs on electricity from a local wind cooperative, and we send our oat fibre to a nearby biogas facility.

Packaging

Our packaging is eco-friendly and fully recyclable!

Farming

We buy our oats from 40 family farms within 200 km of the plant.

We are committed to regenerative farming on every partner farm.

Questions about our ingredients? Write to our customer team and we will answer within two working days.
