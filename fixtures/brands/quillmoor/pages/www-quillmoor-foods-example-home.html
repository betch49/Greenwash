Oat drinks from the north

Quillmoor Foods makes oat drinks and oat yogurts from oats grown on family farms near our plant. Our range includes a barista oat drink, a chocolate oat drink and three oat yogurts.

You can find our products in grocery stores across the region, or order a mixed crate for delivery to your door every two weeks.

Read how we make our products and where our oats come from on our sustainability page.
