Frequently asked questions

Will my repair be covered by the warranty?

When you send your device for repair, the repair center will perform a full inspection and diagnostics. If we find no signs of accidental or cosmetic damage, and your product is still within our standard or extended warranty period, then yes, your repair will be covered!

What if the repair center finds damage not covered by the warranty?

If the repair center finds damage not covered by our warranty, they will send you a quote for the repair costs. You will then have the following options:

  • Accept the quote and pay for the repair.
  • Pay for shipping to have your device returned unrepaired.
  • Abandon the device for free recycling.
How much will my repair cost?

If your repair is covered by our standard or extended warranty, it won’t cost you anything! For repairs not covered by our warranty, the repair center will send you a quote based on our Repair Price lists.

Can I repair my device at home?

Absolutely! We encourage you to repair your Fairphone yourself. To ensure you get the correct solution, we strongly recommend first diagnosing the issue . This will help determine if the repair is covered by your warranty and/or if it's something you can easily fix at home.

Once you've diagnosed the problem, you can explore our detailed guides (above) and find spare parts readily available on our website. If you're still unsure which part to order or how to proceed, our customer support team is always ready to assist you.

Will opening my device cause damage or affect the warranty?

We’ve designed our products to be easily repairable, and if you follow our instructions carefully, performing DIY repairs shouldn’t cause damage or void the warranty.
