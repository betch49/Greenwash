Fairbuds XL Warranty


FairPhone B.V., a Dutch limited liability company located at Van Diemenstraat 200, 1013 CP, Amsterdam, The Netherlands, registered with the Trade Registry number 55901964 (“Fairphone”) warrants to the original purchaser (“You”) that your Fairphone Fairbuds XL (“Product”) is free of any manufacturing defects caused by improper or inferior workmanship, materials, and/or design during the designated warranty period as defined below, and subject to the following terms and conditions, when used normally and in accordance with all operating instructions (“Normal Customer Use”). This Warranty is guaranteed for Fairphone original Products only, and may be extended or supplemented by additional or extended warranty products or services offered by Fairphone.

This Warranty, except where otherwise specified, applies to both generations of Fairbuds XL: Fairbuds XL (2023) [formerly known as Fairbuds XL] and Fairbuds XL [launched on December 9, 2025].

This version of Our Fairbuds XL Warranty terms applies from 09.12.25.

1. The European Union consumer protection and national consumer protection laws

1.1 This warranty statement gives You specific rights, and You may have other rights under the consumer law of your country. The Fairphone Warranty (“Warranty”) does not replace, restrict, limit or otherwise affect your rights under local consumer law or the free of charge, 2-year legal guarantee for all goods provided by Directive 1999/44/EC on certain aspects of the sale of consumer goods and associated guarantees, as amended and supplemented from time to time.

1.2 You can always contact the European Consumer Centre in the country of purchase, or where applicable, the country of residence to obtain detailed information on your rights. For a summary of your rights under EU consumer law visit https://www.fairphone.com/en/legal/

2. Warranty period

2.1 Product Identification & Applicable Warranty

2.1.1 Effective 9 December 2025, the Fairbuds XL launched in 2023 is referred to as Fairbuds XL (2023).

2.1.2 This document applies to both:

    the Fairbuds XL launched in 2023 now marketed as Fairbuds XL (2023), and

    the Fairbuds XL launched on December 9, 2025 (also marketed as "Fairbuds XL”).

These are separate products with different characteristics.

2.1.3 Both Products are covered by Fairphone’s standard 2-year warranty, which applies equally and independently of the product name.

2.1.4 In addition, the Fairbuds XL launched in 2025 come with an extended commercial warranty (“Extended Warranty”), described in Sections 2.3 and 3 of this document.

The Fairbuds XL (2023) do not come with this Extended Warranty.

2.1.5 The applicable coverage (standard warranty only, or standard + Extended Warranty) is determined by the product’s colour variant and (internally) by its SKU and/or serial number.

2.1.6 These identifiers ensure that consumers receive the correct warranty conditions for the specific model purchased and avoid any confusion between generations.

2.1.7 For avoidance of doubt, the distinction is made as follows:

2.1.7.1 Fairbuds XL (2023) Green and Fairbuds XL (2023) Black: standard warranty

2.1.7.2 Fairbuds XL Horizon Black and Fairbuds XL Forest Green: standard + Extended Warranty

2.1.8 Any Fairbuds XL purchased before the renaming is now considered to be designated as Fairbuds XL (2023). Its warranty coverage corresponds to the standard warranty for this product and does not include the extended commercial warranty available for the Fairbuds XL launched in 2025.

2.1.9 This naming clarification does not affect consumers’ EU statutory rights, which remain unchanged.

2.2 Standard Warranty

2.2.1 The Fairphone Fairbuds XL (2023) and Fairbuds XL and replaceable parts (“Spare Parts”) are covered by this Warranty for 24 months from the date of delivery to the original purchaser.

2.2.2 The Warranty in 2.1 shall not apply to the batteries of the Fairphone Fairbuds XL (2023) and Fairbuds XL batteries. The batteries of the Fairphone Fairbuds XL (2023) and Fairbuds XL battery is covered by this Warranty for 12 months from the date of delivery by the original Purchaser.

2.2.3 In case You sell or donate your Product to someone else, this person (“Second Owner”) can benefit from In-Warranty repairs and replacements as long as he/she can show the proof of purchase we or one of our authorized partners issued to You (“Transferred Warranty”). Fairphone does not refund second-hand or donated Fairphone products unless You can demonstrate the price paid for the Product by You, and the original proof of purchase. The transferred Warranty cannot extend beyond the period of Your original Warranty from the date of Your delivery.

2.3 Extended Warranty

2.3.1 Fairphone is proud to be able to offer an extended warranty for the new generation of Fairbuds XL (launched on December 9, 2025). The Extended Warranty applies to only to the aforementioned Product and is not applicable to the Fairbuds XL (2023) independently of their date of purchase.

2.3.2 In the following sections, We will walk You through the nitty gritty of Fairphone’s extended warranty (“Extended Warranty”) so that You can make the best use of it!

After registration, You will receive an email to confirm the information was successfully received. In case you have not received this email following registration, please check Your email spam folder before reaching out to Us. You shall keep the proof of purchase during the period of guarantee and present it in case You wish to claim Your guarantee.

3.2.1.1 If the registration form is incomplete or missing details, you will not be eligible for the Extended Warranty. In case of an error whilst completing the registration form, please contact Our Customer Support as soon as possible.

3.2.1.2 You must provide Us duly, clearly and legibly all conditions necessary for Us to manage Your participation in the Extended Warranty. We are not responsible for errors or omissions in the data You provide.

3.2.1.3 Fairphone reserves the right to check the information provided by You and to disqualify You from participating in the extended warranty, if:

3.3 Extended Warranty terms.

3.3.1 This Extended Warranty for the Fairbuds XL is available to You from 09.12.25 until 31.12.2027 in accordance with the conditions set forth in this article. The duration of Your Extended Warranty will vary depending on the date of registration as outlined in the table below. Fairphone reserves the right to extend or shorten the Extended Warranty for the Fairbuds XL as it deems fit.

3.3.3 Following the original Warranty period, the Extended Warranty will begin based on the date of purchase reflected in Your proof of purchase.

7.1 You can reach out to Fairphone for both in-warranty and out-of-warranty repairs service, however only the in-warranty service shall be free of charge. Warranty service will be limited to the options available in the country where warranty service is requested. Service options, parts availability and response times may vary according to country.

Therefore, please read and follow the instructions in this section, as soon as You experience any defect or malfunction with your Product:

- Review Fairphone’s online customer support website at www.fairphone.com/support for troubleshooting information

  • Ensure You always contact our Customer Support team to receive a Spare part within Your warranty. Please note our Customer Support team may ask you to submit pictures of decent quality of your headphones to assess their condition and whether self-repair is appropriate.
  • Sometimes a Spare part may not function properly due to other causes like software issues. Thus, changing the Spare part might not solve your problem. In case of doubt, please contact Fairphone customer support.
  • Sometimes issues with a Spare part may need to be addressed by our repair center as they require more in depth assessment to verify the condition of the headphones.
  • Although the Fairphone Fairbuds XL is designed for self-repair, it requires careful handling to perform a repair as intended. To ensure safe handling of your Product, we advise You to consider the following:
  1. Ensure that Fairphone customer support or the Fairphone troubleshooting tool advised self-repair of the Fairphone Fairbuds XL.
  2. Carefully review and follow the specific instructions on the support page. These instructions serve as formative documents meant to guide You through the process of opening up your Fairphone Fairbuds XL for repair.
  3. Use the correct tools when taking out or installing Spare Parts. If you take out and install the Spare Part of your Product without the correct tool, this will likely cause damage not covered by this Warranty. For the recommended tools please visit our website for more information.
  4. The Spare Parts are not made to open or take apart. Fairphone Fairbuds XL are headphones which can be repaired by replacing Spare Parts. However opening and/or taking apart the replaceable parts themselves may cause damage and is not covered by this Warranty.

- If your Product is still not functioning properly following the troubleshooting on the support website, you can choose to contact the trader from whom you purchased your Product, contact our customer support team directly through this form), or visit https://www.fairphone.com/en/legal/ to get further information regarding the Fairphone Return Policy.

Fairphone reserves the right to refuse in-warranty service if the proof of purchase is not presented or if it is incomplete/illegible. This warranty shall not apply if the serial number on the product have been altered, defaced or removed.

- Before you send your Fairphone product in for repair, ensure it is carefully packaged (e.g. in the original commercial packaging) and you are advised to remove any additional accessories unless stated otherwise by our Customer Support team Fairphone will remove any remaining material from the Product upon receipt.

- Fairphone does not provide a substitution Product to end-users for the duration of the repair.

- If you send your Product for repair and the Product is not covered by this Warranty or any other consumer rights, Fairphone will inform you of the availability, price, and other conditions applicable to such out-of-warranty repair of the Product. If you do not wish to have the Product repaired, Fairphone will return the Product to you and charge you shipping, handling and diagnostics costs. You may also request Fairphone or its official repair partner to recycle Your headphones free of charge if You do not wish to have the product returned.
