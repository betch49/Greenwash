Business as unusual


We are here to inspire change in the food system because the current one drains resources and fuels climate change. That's why we want to transform the way people think about food and farming, and to consider a future worth working for.
 
For years, the misinformation lobby had people believing that you had to choose between less climate impact and nutrition or great taste. Today, it's common knowledge that you can have all three.
 
We're happy to announce that we're not alone in driving change. Whether you're pouring oat drink in your morning coffee or rethinking your company's climate strategy, you are part of a movement that refuses to settle for business as usual.
